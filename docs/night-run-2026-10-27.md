## Step 1: speed modes
**Tuning → Slow / Medium / Fast.** Each mode sets top speed, acceleration, turn rate and how the sticks feel. The read-only fields show the current mode, its top speed and the current speed.

| | Slow | Medium | Fast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top speed | 0.8 m/s | 2.5 m/s | 6 m/s |
| 20 m flight | 27.5 s | 10.4 s | 6.2 s |
| Max overshoot | 0.44 cm | 0.46 cm | 0.66 cm |
| Max final error | 0.04 cm | 0.11 cm | 0.19 cm |
| Max hover RMS | 0.40 mm | 0.97 mm | 1.54 mm |
| Max tilt | 3.6° | 12.2° | 26.6° |

Yaw error is 0.00° and the largest yaw overshoot is 0.1°. Nothing oscillates: the position error changes sign at most once per move.

**How it works**
- An autonomous flight (Set, B, Y, Pick) no longer jumps the target. It follows a planned reference path from the current state.
  - Short moves use a minimum-jerk quintic.
  - Long moves use a smooth S-curve: ramp up, cruise at top speed, ramp down.
  - Position, velocity and acceleration are fed forward. The acceleration is sampled 50 ms ahead to cover the attitude lag.
- Yaw has its own planned path. Yaw torque is capped at 0.3 N·m, because more saturated the rotors and tilted the drone.
- Gains were retuned once and are shared by all modes. Attitude is Kp 40 / Kd 2.5. The gains are now private, so stale scene values can't override them (that was the cause of the earlier Fast overshoot).
- The integral term only builds up near the reference, which stops windup during transit.

## Step 2: pick one ball
**Tuning → Pick** picks `Tuning.Ball` in Slow mode. The HUD shows `Pick NN <phase>`.

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| Transit | Arm folded. Fly to a hover point 0.24 m behind the ball, on the side away from the bin, with the nose pointing ball → bin. Height 0.52 m. |
| Deploy | Gripper opens to 0.90 (about 7 cm gap). The arm moves to a finger-vertical pose using analytic IK. |
| Descend | The drone lowers to 0.42 m at 0.04 m/s. The arm IK is re-solved every frame from the measured drone pose, so the jaws straddle the ball. |
| Close | Force-limited close: each frame the gripper is commanded to the measured angle − 0.02 (about 1 N·m). A hard squeeze pushed the ball out of the jaw tip. |
| Check | Grip angle must be in [0.30, 0.60] and the ball within 3 cm of the grasp site and centred on the pads. If not: open, redeploy, retry up to 2 times. |
| Lift / Carry | The arm is frozen. The drone climbs so the lowest point clears the rim by at least 10 cm, then flies the ball over the bin centre. |
| Drop / Retreat | Gripper opens and the ball falls in. The drone climbs away. |

Ball 14, in 10 runs from `reset`: **10/10**, about 34 s each. The bin moved 0.00 cm, the ball came within 0.1 cm of the bin centre, and max tilt was 5.4°.

The design was first prototyped offline in Python MuJoCo (58/58 runs) and then ported to C#. The offsets from the prototype worked unchanged in Lucky.

## Step 2.5: bin keep-out
Every autonomous flight goes through one check against the **live** bin pose. Covered: Pick, Set/B, Y, `goto`, and the test moves.
- **Zone:** bin radius + 0.5 m + the drone's current horizontal reach, which counts the rotors, the arm as posed, and a held ball.
- **Rule:** inside the zone, the lowest point of drone + arm + ball stays at least 10 cm above the rim. A straight leg that would break the rule becomes climb → cruise → descend.
- **Goals inside the zone** that are too low are raised, and the drone logs `METRIC keepout clamp`.

**Tests (`test bin`):** low crossings from 4 directions and on the diagonal, chords near the edge, goals next to the bin, crossings with the arm deployed, a drone parked low beside the bin, Set to the balls nearest the bin, and picks right after a drop.
- 28–34 cases. Minimum clearance was 13 cm wherever the rule applies; the parked-low starts read 10.1 cm at the moment the rule first holds.
- The bin moved 0.00 cm in every case. Bin-wall contact above 1 N appeared in 0 cases, except one single frame in one later run.

## Step 3: Pick All
**Tuning → Pick All** (or `pickall [seed]`).
1. Shuffles the balls not yet in the bin and flies to a random start pose and heading.
2. Picks each ball from its live position, going straight from the drop to the next ball.
3. Folds the arm before any yaw turn. It never turns with the arm low.
4. Re-plans if a ball rolled more than 2 cm.
5. Requeues a dropped ball once; anything else is skipped with a logged reason.

| Test | Result | Time | Bin moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each ball alone from `reset` | 25/25, no retries | 28–40 s each | 0.00 cm |
| Pick All, seed 1 | 25/25 | 983 s | 0.00 cm |
| Pick All, seed 2 | 25/25 | 985 s | 0.00 cm |
| Pick All, seed 3 | 25/25 | 982 s | 0.01 cm |

All 25 balls are 1.15–4.9 m from the bin and at least 0.38 m apart. Minimum clearance over the rim was 13.7 cm.

## Dev command file
Write lines into `LuckyEngine/Cache/nightrun_cmd.txt` during Play. It is polled 4 times a second and cleared after each read. Every result line starts with `METRIC` in `/tmp/LuckyEngine/Logs/APP.log`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `mode slow\|medium\|fast` | Sets the speed mode |
| `reset` | Resets the scene |
| `goto x y z [yaw]` | Flies to a point, MuJoCo frame |
| `ball N`, `set` | Selects a ball and flies to it |
| `pick N [a\|b]` | Picks ball N; `a` stops after lowering, `b` after lift |
| `pickall [seed]` | Picks every ball |
| `test flight` | Flight test suite |
| `test bin` | Bin keep-out test suite |

## Known limits
- **Not checked by eye:** how the Slow / Medium / Fast buttons render in the Tuning panel. Screenshots only capture the 3D viewport.
- **Not tested:** manual stick flight in each mode, because no controller was connected. The code path was reviewed.
- **Not covered by the keep-out:** manual flight and G (arm deploy) near the bin.
- **Never triggered:** grasp retry, requeue and rolled-ball re-plan. Every grasp succeeded first time, and the sim is deterministic, so seeds only change the order and the start pose.
- **Bin-wall contact metric:** it can't tell drone contacts from ball contacts. Bin displacement is the backup metric.
- **Lucky with a locked screen:** Lucky crashes (Vulkan `AcquireNextImage2KHR ... result = 2`) unless it is launched with `MESA_VK_WSI_PRESENT_MODE=mailbox`. This only matters for unattended runs.
